# Lab Meetings

## Weekly Lab Meetings

Lab meetings are held in person every Monday from 10:00am-11:30am in the OPG building. These serve as a forum for lab members to present their research, discuss ongoing projects, and share updates. Attendance is expected for all lab members, and active participation is encouraged.

An agenda is available on our [Google Drive](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nZMBdWCSWIrWoGlAaSIPEiA3OZWKsifJN2AKUqH8O2o/edit?usp=drive_link). Please update the agenda with your presentation title and any relevant materials. Even if you are not presenting, you can add items to the agenda for discussion.

## One-on-One Meetings

One-on-one meetings are scheduled weekly between each lab member and Kevin. These meetings provide an opportunity to discuss individual progress, address any concerns, and set goals for the upcoming weeks. Please contact Kevin to schedule a meeting.
