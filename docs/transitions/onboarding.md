# Onboarding

Welcome to the Wang lab! The following document will help you with all your onboarding needs.

**Important**: We assume that you have completed onboarding through UHN before starting in the Wang lab.

**Are you a rotation student?** Please note that some of the steps below may not be applicable to you. Please reach out to Kevin or Farnoosh if you have any questions.

## Request access to Lab Resources
Farnoosh can assist you with getting access to the following resources. Please reach out to her if you need help with any of these steps.

1. Request Google Drive Access
2. Join our Slack
3. Join our [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/wanglab-cns)

In addition, you will need to request access to the following computing resources:

1. Request access to [DRAC](../resources/drac.md#getting-access-to-drac-and-nibi)
2. Request access to [H4H](../resources/h4h.md)

## Meetings
1. Attend your [first lab meeting](../general/lab-meetings.md)
2. Schedule your first [one-on-one meeting with Kevin](../general/lab-meetings.md)
3. Schedule an onboarding meeting with an existing lab member to go over lab policies and procedures

## Miscellaneous
1. Add your information for the lab website to the lab member list in the [Google Drive](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1b77LOQ123YNYB_sYZ8JGT4Di5W_yVQKSYaNbSHTOJLk/edit?usp=sharing)
2. Familiarize yourself with DRAC and H4H computing resources. See the [DRAC](../resources/drac.md) and [H4H](../resources/h4h.md) pages for more information.
